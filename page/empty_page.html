What this shows

    
Given nothing to continue, a base model writes whatever its training made most typical. These runs show that
    default for each model: its prior over documents, before any prompt shapes it.

    
How the runs were made

    
Every model got its own start-of-document token and nothing else. Sampling was plain: temperature 1, no top-k
    or top-p, no repetition penalty, 256 new tokens, stopping early only at the end-of-text token. The classes and
    the predictions were registered before any run was drawn. Contact details are redacted, and the
     runs omitted for content appear as black squares.

    
How the runs were classified

    
A language model () classified every run, with the writing model's name hidden. It gave
    each run one class and quoted the words behind it, and the panel under the grid underlines that quote. Before any
    result was read,  runs were classified by hand, also with names hidden, and the two
    classifications were compared. They agreed on  of those runs about whether a run is
    assistant-era. Across all five classes, Cohen's κ was . Human-written questions and answers,
    such as forum threads and exam banks, appear in grey and do not count as assistant-era.
