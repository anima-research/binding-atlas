Each point is one FFN neuron at one depth: 294,912 in all. They are placed by what they respond to in 24M tokens of the model's own training text. The model, its training run and every measurement here come from one project, from random initialisation onward.

Placed by what they fire on, they fall into 400 groups and 9 families

A check on the map

Hand-validated neurons, and where the map put them

These neurons were characterised by hand before the map existed (firing screens, steering, forked log-probabilities). The map never saw those results. The loop gives a free test: a neuron's two passes share weights, so if the map tracks function, the two copies should land in the same group.

Method, and what to trust

How it was made

Firing pass24M tokens of the model's training data through all 72 × 4,096 neurons; the 24 strongest and weakest firings of each, and running means.
Three readingsSentence embeddings of each neuron's firing contexts; the tokens its output vector promotes (logit lens and J-lens); the words that appear when it is steered.
Map and names400 groups by k-means on the embeddings, a 2-D layout by UMAP, then language-model agents named each group from its exemplars and graded the agreement of the readings.
Binding attributionFor 4,440 binding-battery items, activation × gradient of the margin between the right candidate and the strongest wrong one, for every neuron at every position.
Knock-outsSets chosen on training items, tested on held-out items: each neuron's activation replaced by its corpus mean. Controls are random neurons at the same positions.
