Who Is You? · take it yourself

Who is you? Read it the way a model has to.

By Luxia (@kataletheia), with Claude (Anthropic) · compute and hosting by Anima Labs

Each item is a short scene: people talk, objects change hands, and a line stops just before its last word. You choose or type that word. The items are the same kind of text that language models are scored on in the Binding Atlas, drawn fresh for every reader. Many models fail some of them, even very large ones, and a reader's answers show which ones people find easy.

Take as many or as few as you like. Read each one once, the way you would read a message, and answer. Nothing tells you whether an answer was right until you ask for your summary. No name, address or account is stored: only your answers, how long each took, and a random id kept in this browser.

Answers after you open the summary are kept apart.

The items read like unfinished text on purpose: a model sees exactly this, and must continue it.
